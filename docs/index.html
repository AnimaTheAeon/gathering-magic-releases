Gathering Magic

Fast, offline card search and deck building for Magic: The Gathering players.

Contact

Found a problem or have an idea? In the app, open Settings → Help and feedback and send a message straight to the developer. You can also open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

Why do I need to download card data?
Gathering Magic keeps the whole card list on your phone so searching is instant and works offline. The first download comes from Scryfall and takes a few minutes on Wi-Fi.

How do I get new cards?
Tap the refresh button on the Search tab, or Update card data in Settings. The app only downloads new data when you ask.

How do I search card text?
Type any words from a card's name or rules text, in any order. Put a phrase in quotes to match it exactly, like "draw a card".

How do I keep my decks safe?
In Settings, tap Save backup and choose Files, iCloud Drive or email. On a new phone, use Restore file.

What are .dck files?
A plain-text deck list. Save one from a deck's menu, or open a .dck file from Files, email or another app to import it.
