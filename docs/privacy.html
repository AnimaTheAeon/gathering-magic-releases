Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Gathering Magic does not collect, store or share any personal data, apart from a feedback message if you choose to send one (see below). There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your decks, favorites, wishlist and settings are saved on your device (and in your own backups, if you make them). The developer never receives them.

Card data from Scryfall

To show cards, the app downloads card data and card images directly from Scryfall (scryfall.com). Like any website you visit, Scryfall's servers receive your device's IP address when these files are downloaded. No other information about you is sent. See Scryfall's privacy policy for how they handle requests.

Backups and shared decks

When you save a backup or share a deck, the file goes only where you choose, such as Files, iCloud Drive, email or another app. That service's own privacy policy then applies.

If you turn on Automatic backup, a backup file is saved after each change to a place that belongs to you: your own iCloud Drive on iPhone, or a folder you pick on Android or Windows. It's never sent to the developer.

Tips

Tips are optional. On iPhone they're handled entirely by Apple's In-App Purchase; on Android and Windows, the tip button opens the developer's Buy Me a Coffee page in your browser. The app never sees your payment details. Apple or Buy Me a Coffee handles the payment under its own privacy policy.

Feedback you send

If you use Help and feedback in the app's Settings, the name, email address and message you type are sent to the developer by email (through Formspree, a form service), along with the app version and type of device. They're used only to reply to you and to fix problems, and are never shared or sold. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from children. The only information it ever sends is a feedback message that someone chooses to send.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
